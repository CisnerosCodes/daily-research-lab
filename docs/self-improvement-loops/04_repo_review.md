# Internal review: the 56 in-repo experiments, 2026-07-21 through 2026-08-13

Written 2026-08-13 by a dedicated review agent (Claude Opus) that read `registry.jsonl`, `docs/SYSTEM.md`, `docs/DAILY_TASK.md`, `docs/BACKLOG.md`, both long arcs in full and in date order, roughly twenty further experiment READMEs, and several `results.json` files to check claimed effects against reported seed spreads. Part of the self-improvement-loops research update; companion documents in this folder.

## 1. What works

The repo has a genuine methodological identity, and it is not the obvious one. The obvious reading is "tiny experiments, fast turnaround." The actual engine is something narrower and better: **almost every strong row is built around a named control arm whose purpose is to kill a deflationary explanation of the headline effect.** That is a habit most small labs never acquire and many large ones perform only under review pressure.

**The chance-floor control.** `2026-07-25_zoology-mqar-recall` is the exemplar and probably the most quietly important row in the repo. The pre-registered question was whether a decay-gated linear mixer falls off a capacity cliff or degrades gracefully on MQAR. The answer is neither, and the reason is that the experiment computed three query-ignoring baselines (random present value, modal present value, most recent value) and found the mixer sitting on them to within ±0.04 at every load from N=2. The apparently graceful roughly-1/N decay that a normal write-up would have called a capacity curve is the guessing floor. The experiment then says so explicitly: "anyone sweeping KV load against a sub-quadratic mixer should plot that baseline before claiming a capacity curve." An experiment that dissolves its own question and produces a reusable rule for reading other people's plots is worth more than one that answers it.

**Matched-parametrization information controls.** `2026-07-29_mqar-gate-noise-control` is the cleanest single design in the repo. The dense forget gate rescues MQAR recall; the deflationary stories are that the gate is extra trainable machinery, extra gradient dimensionality through the decay path, or a stochastic perturbation that shakes the model off a plateau. The experiment holds all of those fixed and degrades only the information: `shufgate` is the identical gate with identical parameter count and identical activation statistics fed the batch-shuffled sequence, and `noisegate` is train-time-only Gaussian logit noise. Both are exact no-ops (Δ +0.001 and −0.002) while the real gate produces a +0.65 swing. The sanity arm confirms both controls still solve N=4, so the impairment is specific to the recall circuit rather than to learning in general. That is a textbook design and it transfers directly to any gated architecture.

The same instinct recurs across unrelated tracks and is what produces most of the sharp rows:

- `2026-07-26_snn-spiking` adds a `constant` arm that injects the analog pixel as a current rather than encoding it as spikes. It beats both rate and latency coding at every timestep count and matches an iso-parameter ReLU MLP at T=1, which reframes the entire rate-versus-latency debate as a fight over how to pay for a self-inflicted wound.
- `2026-07-26_cfc-liquid-timeseries` runs the two-by-two that the liquid-network literature does not: a CfC fed constant `dt` and a plain GRU with `dt` appended. The whole advantage is the `dt` channel and none of it is the closed form.
- `2026-07-26_data-repetition-epochs` finds that ninety percent of an apparent data-repetition penalty is corpus coverage, because the obvious design (contiguous prefixes) confounds repetition with vocabulary coverage. The confound was nine times larger than the effect being measured.
- `2026-07-26_othello-probe-vs-cause` measures every intervention against a norm-matched random edit and reports collateral damage alongside effect size, which is what exposes that the headline `strict` intervention number is bought with magnitude rather than content.

**Iso-parameter and iso-FLOP discipline, taken to the point of bit-identity.** `2026-07-26_head-dim-vs-count-isoparam` is the best instance because the control is exact by construction rather than approximate: at fixed `d_model`, the attention block's parameter count does not depend on the head split at all, so all six arms have literally one parameter count, identical module shapes, bit-identical initial weights per seed, and the same batch stream. `2026-07-25_shadow-loop-vs-depth-isoflop` adds a built-in falsification check — tied-k1 and untied-k1 are the same model and must be bit-identical, so the reported gap at k=1 is required to be exactly 0.0. `2026-07-25_zoology-mqar-recall` binary-searches `d_ff` per mixer to hit 100k parameters within a 0.198 percent spread. These are not decorative; the 0.0 sanity gap and the exact parameter equality are what make the reported deltas interpretable at all at this scale.

**Seed noise reported as a denominator rather than an afterthought.** The strong rows consistently report the effect as a multiple of the seed floor: `head-dim-vs-count-isoparam` gives config spread 0.217 bpc against a within-config seed spread of 0.0225, a ratio of 9.65; `swiglu-vs-gelu-isoparam` reports the win as three seed-widths; `retnet-nano-char` reports the decay schedule as worth 12.7 times seed noise. This is the correct primary statistic for a lab operating below the scale where absolute numbers mean anything.

**Paired inits.** The single best methodological escalation in the repo, and it happened mid-stream. From `2026-08-01_mqar-gate-freeze-before-breakout` onward (and independently in the qknorm arc from `2026-07-30`), every arm within a seed starts byte-identical and sees the identical data stream, so trajectories are identical up to the intervention and any later divergence is caused by the intervention alone. The 08-01 README states the reasoning explicitly and notes that the previous night's `frozen1000` arm could not do this because its arm name entered the init seed. This converts a noisy three-seed comparison into a paired test and is why the freeze-step monotonicity can be called causal rather than suggestive.

**The refuted-hypothesis-with-a-mechanism genre.** This is the repo's house style and it produces its best writing. Three exemplars:

`2026-07-25_grokking-modular-addition` is the strongest single experiment in the corpus. It refutes its own pre-registered progress measure (spectral entropy lags the test-accuracy jump by 876 to 936 steps rather than leading it), and then does two further things that most published negative results do not. First, it explains why the opposite is reported in the literature: re-analyzing the same run as if training had been truncated at the jump flips every sign, because normalizing by fraction-of-total-movement when the denominator is still growing manufactures a lead out of a lag. Second, it runs a false-positive control on the train-fraction-0.3 run that never generalizes at all, where the entropy still falls by 0.98 bits. A refutation, a mechanism for the erroneous literature, and a false-positive rate, in one ten-minute experiment.

`2026-07-25_dyck-probe-can-lie` is strong for a different reason: the mechanism was stated in advance rather than reverse-engineered. In any Dyck prefix, depth is congruent to position modulo two, so depth parity is decodable for free from the positional embedding while carrying zero information about the answer. The experiment predicts a 99.5-percent-accurate probe whose certified LEACE erasure costs less than a random direction of the same rank, and gets exactly that (−0.16 nats against `top_type`'s +5.31 at matched probe accuracy). It also reports two honest misses against its own pre-registration, including `top_recency`, which it diagnoses as collateral damage on an entangled direction rather than quietly dropping.

`2026-07-31_qknorm-hd4-temperature-rescue` refutes the lab's own mechanism story from the previous experiment, and the refutation's mechanism is the interesting part: handed a free per-head sharpness dial, gradient descent leaves it at tau ≈ 1.04 when it would need roughly 2.6 to match the baseline's trained logit scale. The model is not straining against a bound; the loss gradient simply does not point toward sharper attention. That observation is more interesting than the rescue would have been.

**Multi-day causal arcs that genuinely build.** The qknorm sequence `2026-07-26_head-dim-vs-count-isoparam` → `07-30` → `07-31` → `08-02` → `08-06` is the most complete object in the repo. It starts from a flagged confound in a parent experiment, uses QK-norm as a *control* rather than as a technique, splits one monotone curve into two distinct taxes, refutes the first proposed mechanism causally, confirms the second one partially, and closes it with a gradient-path manipulation — producing a decomposition of a single 0.130-bpc effect into 2 percent static temperature, 54 percent detached per-token feature, and 98 percent once the gradient path is open, with a `raw r_t` control at 4 percent that rules out the trivial reading. Each step is a one-variable change on a byte-compatible harness whose replication anchors land to the fourth or fifth decimal. That is a real causal decomposition, and it is rare at any scale.

**Self-correction is logged rather than silently overwritten.** `2026-07-27_mqar-feature-map-vs-width` takes down its own parent's headline from the previous day and says so in the title area of the takeaway. `2026-08-11_qknorm-dyntemp-composite-sweep` declines to claim "strictly better" when the sweep does not support it. `2026-07-26_looped-halt-nrasp` explicitly corrects an earlier registry row. Very few labs publish "our result from six days ago was an artifact of our own protocol."

## 2. What is weak

**Everything is one scale, and mostly one corpus.** Parameter counts run from 14 to about 424k. Thirteen or so language rows all use tiny-shakespeare at the character level; everything else is synthetic and generated in-process. This is a legitimate constraint, and the READMEs are usually honest about it in the caveats — but the honesty lives in paragraph six while the titles and the index make scale-free claims. The registry headline for `2026-07-26_head-dim-vs-count-isoparam` reads "one giant head wins," while the README's own honest reading two paragraphs later is that head_dim ≥ 64 is flat, the two seeds disagree about which of the top two wins, and the difference (0.011 bpc) is well inside the 0.064 seed range of the arm it is being compared against. The index is where an outside reader stops, and the index systematically overstates.

The claims most exposed on scale, specifically:

- **The entire post-`07-30` qknorm arc lives at a degenerate configuration.** The cliff config is `head_dim=4, n_head=32` at `d_model=128`. No production model occupies that corner; head dimensions below 32 are essentially unheard of, and the arc's central object — a 0.130-bpc penalty at head_dim 4 — may be a fact about four-dimensional query spaces rather than about QK-norm. `2026-08-11` is the row that partially tests this and it finds the mechanism does *not* generalize to the other end of the sweep (the nh=1 tax has a different cause the channel cannot reach). That is exactly the kind of result that predicts the mid-range behaviour at realistic head dimensions is also a different regime.
- **`2026-07-25_pe-length-gen-tiny`** reports the positional-encoding length-generalization ranking inverting at 0.1M parameters relative to the published 107M result. The README concedes the binding caveat — iso-compute rather than to-convergence, one task — but "the ranking inverts at tiny scale" and "NoPE is slow to learn and this is an iso-step comparison" are very different claims, and only the second is supported. NoPE losing twice at small compute is precisely what an undertrained comparison looks like.
- **Nearly every language-model row is undertrained by its own admission**, typically sitting at 2.7 to 3.2 bits per character against a converged tiny char LM's roughly 1.5 to 1.7. What is being measured throughout is *fitting speed at a fixed small budget*, not converged quality. `swiglu-vs-gelu-isoparam`, `head-dim-vs-count-isoparam`, the whole qknorm arc (600 steps), `shadow-recursion-capacity-window` (400 steps), and `byte-vs-bpe-isoflops` all say this. The critical point is that the repo has run the fix exactly once — phase 2 of `2026-07-27_mqar-feature-map-vs-width`, at 10x budget — and it overturned a headline, revealing that a "pinned cliff" was a 15,000-step optimization plateau. The single experiment that tested the pervasive caveat found the caveat was load-bearing. Yet the ten-times-steps follow-up is proposed in at least eight READMEs and executed in one.

**Underpowered cells where the effect is marginal.** The repo usually reports seed spread; it does not always respect it.

- `2026-08-11_qknorm-dyntemp-composite-sweep` is the clearest case. Its verdict rule uses a ±0.015 bpc tolerance, but the measured seed spreads at n=2 in `results.json` are 0.0637 (baseline, hd=64) and 0.0671 (composite, hd=64) — four times the tolerance. The row nonetheless records hd=64 as "OK (beats both)" on a −0.016 margin against QK-norm and −0.057 against baseline. Those intervals overlap completely. The hd=32 cell is the same story at smaller magnitude (baseline spread 0.030, claimed comparisons at 0.002). The hd=128 "FAILS" verdict rests on +0.037 against an 0.018 baseline spread with two seeds. The README does flag the hd=64 spread in its caveats, which is to its credit, but the verdict table it publishes does not, and the verdict table is what will be quoted. Two seeds is not enough to run a five-cell decision rule at 0.015 resolution, and the row's own data says so.
- `2026-07-26_group-grokking` reports a delay ratio of at least 75x and a clean 4/4-versus-0/5 abelian/non-abelian split, from cells of n=1, with the non-abelian arms censored by a wall-clock cap while still rising. The README's caveat section is exemplary and states all of this plainly. The registry headline does not, and a "≥75x" ratio computed against a censored single-seed run is not a ratio, it is a lower bound with unknown support.
- `2026-07-25_filler-vs-recur` is one seed with ±0.02 matched-FLOP margins and correctly says only the large effects survive plausible seed variance — but its headline claim is precisely a *tie* between pause tokens and recurrence, and a tie is the one claim a single seed cannot support.
- `2026-07-26_mup-lr-transfer` runs one seed and its central quantitative claim (residual drift of −0.38 octaves per doubling under muP) sits at the resolution of the ±0.2-octave wobble the README itself names as unbounded.
- `2026-07-26_byte-vs-bpe-isoflops` is the model of how to handle this — it declares both legs inconclusive, names the seed SD that causes it (0.109 against the other arm's 0.020), and reports only the decomposition that is sign-stable. That row makes the qknorm sweep's handling of the same problem look worse by comparison, not better.

**Post-hoc mechanism stories.** The repo is unusually good about this, which is why the lapses are diagnosable rather than pervasive.

- The clearest instance is the one the repo caught itself: `2026-07-30_qknorm-head-dim` inferred a "sharpness cap" mechanism from post-hoc probes (entropy 0.90, top-1 weight 0.107) and `2026-07-31` refuted it causally within a day. The system worked, but the episode shows how readily the mechanism paragraph gets written the same night on correlational probe evidence.
- The live instance is `2026-08-13_mqar-joint-lr-scaling`. Its headline decomposition attributes 43 percent of an observed sublinearity to gate-lag and the remaining 57 percent to "the optimizer/curvature side." That residual is, by construction, everything not otherwise explained, given a name. Weight decay is offered as a "named suspect" and the follow-up is queued, which is honest — but the row's abstract-level claim is a two-way decomposition in which one of the two terms is unmeasured.
- `2026-08-07_mqar-qkv-lr-ratio` reports that attention-circuit travel at escape is "eerily tight within-arm" at CV below 0.5 percent. That is three numbers (49.29, 49.63, 49.29), and the interpretation offered around it is not tested.
- `2026-07-25_zoology-mqar-recall` promotes two findings from *pilots* rather than from controlled sweeps — that batch size is the binding constraint on recall-circuit formation, and that embedding initialization standard deviation is load-bearing — and explicitly frames them as generalizing beyond the item. They may well be true and they are useful engineering notes, but they are uncontrolled observations presented with the same confidence as the controlled results.

**Missing baselines, and one specific missing experiment that the repo keeps naming.** The MQAR arc discovered twice, on 07-27 and again on 07-28, that its own fixed-budget "solved at 0.9 accuracy in 2000 steps" criterion is an artifact that can order gate variants essentially arbitrarily — its own table would rank rank1 above rank4 on a difference the row itself attributes to init noise. The named fix is backlog item 51, `mqar-gate-escape-time`: rank × budget {2k, 8k, 32k} × at least five seeds, escape time as primary metric. That item has sat unstarted since 07-28 while six further MQAR experiments were built on top of the unfixed protocol, all at 2000 steps with three seeds and a 100-step eval grid. The arc knows its instrument is miscalibrated and kept using it.

**Negative results: mostly informative, sometimes just underpowered.** The good ones carry a control that distinguishes "did not learn within budget" from "cannot represent" — the no-recall baseline in `zoology-mqar-recall`, the tau-stayed-at-init probe in `qknorm-hd4-temperature-rescue`, the N=4 sanity arms in `mqar-gate-noise-control`. The weaker ones do not. `2026-07-25_shadow-halt-entropy-tiny` reports that entropy-based adaptive depth buys nothing, at 1800 steps, on a task whose quality-versus-depth curve is flat — and the README concedes the precondition (measure the fixed-k curve first; it is one line and would have predicted the null in advance). That is a null about the task, not about halting. `2026-07-26_cfc-liquid-timeseries` reports indistinguishability at two seeds with a learning rate selected at the grid edge for every arm.

**Structural and bookkeeping observations, which bear on how the whole corpus should be read.** Forty-one of the fifty-six rows are dated 2026-07-25 or 2026-07-26 — twenty-one and twenty respectively. The README's framing of "one small experiment per day" and "56 experiments logged" does not describe the record: two backfill days produced three quarters of the corpus, and the genuine daily cadence is the fifteen experiments from 07-27 to 08-13, which itself is missing 08-04, 08-05, 08-08, 08-09, 08-10 and 08-12. Separately, all fifty-six rows carry `status: done`; there is not one `failed` row, despite `docs/DAILY_TASK.md` explicitly budgeting for them and calling them valuable. Either nothing ever failed, which is not credible across fifty-six attempts, or failures went unlogged — and an experiment log with a zero failure rate has a selection effect in it. Finally, fifty-three of fifty-six novelty checks record the scripted arXiv and OpenAlex endpoints returning HTTP 403, meaning `scripts/novelty_check.py` has essentially never run as designed and every novelty verdict in the registry is a self-assessment from manual web search. That is not fatal — the prior-art sections are genuinely well researched, often with fetched abstracts — but the SYSTEM.md pipeline described as the lab's defense against redundancy is not the pipeline that ran.

## 3. The arc structure

Both arcs are self-improvement loops in a precise sense: each day's design is a function of the previous day's residual, and neither arc consulted an external question after its second entry.

**The variation operator** is: *take the single largest unexplained residual in yesterday's results table and convert it into a one-variable intervention on a byte-identical harness.* Three sub-operators recur:

1. *Deflationary-control generation* — "could this be something less interesting?" This produces `shufgate` and `noisegate` (07-29), the `raw r_t` arm (08-06), the `static` and `scalar` input-independence arms (07-28), and the input-independent decay control that separates learned decay from selectivity.
2. *Dose-response and timing* — "when, and how much?" This produces the freeze-step sweep (08-01), the gate-LR multiplier sweep (08-03), the backbone-LR mirror (08-07), and the joint arm (08-13).
3. *Scope extension* — "does it hold off the one cell we have been standing on?" This produces the full width sweep (07-27) and the full head-split sweep (08-11). This operator fires rarely, and both times it fired it changed the story.

**The selection criterion** is not accuracy and not novelty. It is *separation on paired inits*: an arm is kept if it produces a gap that is visibly outside the harness's own seed behaviour, and the whole design apparatus (shared init keys, shared batch streams, frozen harnesses) exists to make that judgement cheap. A secondary criterion is that replication anchors must land — every row in both arcs reruns its parent's arms and reports agreement to the third, fourth or fifth decimal, and those anchors are checked before the new result is interpreted.

**What accumulates** is four things, in decreasing order of value. First, and most valuable, a **ledger of retired explanations**: state size, optimization conditioning, gradient dimensionality, stochastic perturbation, travel-as-clock, gate-as-throttle, static temperature, key-side magnitude, raw magnitude restoration. The arcs' real product is elimination, not construction. Second, **the frozen harness itself**, which is what makes fourth-decimal replication anchors possible and therefore makes single-variable attribution credible. Third, **methodological rules that migrate across arcs** — paired inits were forced by the MQAR arc's 07-28 init-lottery embarrassment and appear in the qknorm arc from 07-30 onward; "escape time, not endpoint"; "plot the chance baseline"; "single-seed cells adjacent to a cliff are untrustworthy." Fourth, **the backlog**, where unspent branches are parked, with the qualification noted above that the most important parked branch has stayed parked.

**Where the loop made real progress.**

The MQAR arc's high-yield stretch is 07-25 through 07-29, five experiments and five genuinely distinct conclusions. 07-25 establishes that the sub-quadratic curves are the chance floor. 07-26 produces the width-invariance puzzle (the cliff does not move across a 4x sweep of `d_head`). 07-27 dissolves the puzzle by showing that fixed-step frontiers measure plateau-escape time rather than capacity, restoring a capacity law under the Taylor kernel (N* = d_head/4) and simultaneously demolishing its own parent's headline. 07-28 finds that the gate-rank axis is a cliff rather than a ramp and that everything below full rank is a no-op or an init lottery. 07-29 nails the mechanism with two matched-parametrization controls. Every one of those five changes what you would do next; 07-27 in particular is the highest-information single step in either arc, because it retroactively reinterprets everything before it.

The qknorm arc's productive stretch is 07-30 through 08-06 and it is tighter still: split one effect into two taxes, refute the obvious mechanism causally, confirm the real one at 54 percent, close it at 98 percent with a null control that rules out the trivial version. Four experiments, one complete causal decomposition, with a practical artifact at the end.

**Where it spiralled.**

The MQAR arc's failure mode is visible in one sentence: **after 07-27 it stopped varying N and d.** From 07-28 onward, six consecutive experiments (07-28, 07-29, 08-01, 08-03, 08-07, 08-13) run at literally one cell — N=8, d=64, d_head=32, 86k parameters, 2000 steps, elu+1, dense gate. The question quietly migrated from "what limits recall in linear attention" to "what sets the escape step of this specific model at this specific cell." By 08-13 the headline is a decomposition of a 1.45x residual in Adam's learning-rate reparametrization on one plateau. That is a claim about AdamW, not about linear attention, and the arc cannot generalize it because it has exactly one plateau to look at. Worse, the arc's own 07-27 lesson was precisely that single-cell results adjacent to a plateau boundary are init lotteries — and every finding from 08-01 onward is unreplicated at any other (N, d, kernel) point.

The qknorm arc's 08-11 is the healthy version of the same instinct. It went and checked the whole curve, and the check caught a real overclaim: the composite is strictly better than QK-norm but not strictly better than attention, because it inherits a single-head tax with a different mechanism. But the scope extension was under-resourced relative to the precision its own verdict rule demanded — two seeds, on cells whose seed spread the same file measures at 0.06.

**Marginal information yield.** Taking `2026-07-27` as the unit (it retired an explanation class, produced a quantitative law, and invalidated its parent), rough estimates:

**The diagnosis that generalizes.** The qknorm arc decayed gracefully and the MQAR arc did not, for a structural reason worth writing into `SYSTEM.md`: **the qknorm arc had a bounded quantity to explain.** The cliff is 0.130 bpc; each experiment refunds a measurable fraction of it; when the fractions sum to 98 percent the arc is over, and the arc correctly recognized this and moved to scope-checking. The MQAR arc lost its denominator after 07-29. Once the target became "why is this scaling 2.1x rather than 4x," there was no termination condition — any residual can be decomposed into a smaller residual indefinitely. An arc should be started only when someone can state, on day one, what number would mean it is finished.

## 4. Which findings could turn heads

Ranked by how likely a researcher at a large lab is to stop scrolling, and what each would need.

**1. The three-row ground-truth verdict on sparse autoencoders.** `2026-07-25_sae-on-grokked-model`, `2026-07-25_sae-on-merged-pairs`, and `2026-07-26_tracr-ground-truth-lab`. On three separate problems where the true features are provable rather than assumed, the untouched basis beats the learned dictionary: 0.75 versus 0.32 frequency purity on the grokked model, eight merged directions and zero true features on the toy model, and 29/29 versus 0/29 on Tracr. The Tracr row then does the thing that lifts this from a complaint to a result — an independence control with the same 29 axes and the same firing rates scores 23/29, which pins the failure on feature *dependence* rather than density, dictionary size, or superposition. The grokked-model row supplies the complementary mechanism (true Fourier features are dense at 62.6 percent firing, and the L1 prior rewards rarity, so the two are in direct conflict), plus the warning that decoder-cosine scoring has a 0.482 random-direction null and that naive max-cosine scoring fakes 15/16 recovery via duplicates.

*What it needs:* a top-k, JumpReLU, or matryoshka arm, because "you used L1, of course it failed" is the first sentence of every review and the repo already knows it — this is the highest-value single follow-up in the entire lab. At least one non-toy site, even a small real GPT's MLP, so the claim is not purely about hand-built ground truth. Seeds on the purity numbers. And it needs to be written as one benchmark paper — "a ground-truth answer-key kit for scoring dictionary methods, and the three ways they fail on it" — rather than three separate negative rows.

**2. QK-norm severs a learnable per-token sharpness channel.** `2026-07-31` + `2026-08-02` + `2026-08-06`, with `2026-08-11` as the scope check. The ladder — static per-head temperature refunds 2 percent, detached per-token query-RMS temperature refunds 54 percent, undetached refunds 98 percent, raw undetached restoration refunds 4 percent — is a real causal decomposition with a null control at each end, and it terminates in a concrete parametrization that dominates plain QK-norm across the multi-head sweep. The subsidiary observations are independently interesting: the win is modulation variance rather than mean sharpness (entropy essentially unchanged at 0.896 versus 0.902), the model keeps the dial engaged where it helps and disengages it monotonically where it cannot (alpha 0.98 → 0.66 across the head split), and key-side restoration actively hurts.

*What it needs:* this is the most scale-sensitive claim in the repo and the one where a reviewer's objection is strongest. It needs at least `d_model` 512 to 1024 with head dimensions in the 32 to 128 range that real models use, because the cliff config (head_dim 4, 32 heads, `d_model` 128) is a corner nobody occupies, and 08-11 already shows the mechanism does not cover the other end of its own sweep. It needs training past the 600-step early regime; every number in the arc is a fitting-speed number. It needs a second corpus. And it needs reframing: not "the hd=4 cliff," which sounds like a curiosity, but "QK-norm removes a per-token temperature channel that the model uses, here is a two-line fix, and here is the head-dimension regime where it costs you."

**3. Probe accuracy is anti-correlated with causal use on a world model.** `2026-07-26_othello-probe-vs-cause`. The world model forms and is causally used (0.826 direction-metric success against a 0.491 norm-matched control at a 10-percent-norm edit), but a 0.098 spread in per-cell probe accuracy hides a 36x spread in per-cell causal effect, and the three-way probe metric the Othello-GPT literature actually reports is anti-correlated with causal effect at r = −0.594. The mechanism is geometric and stated plainly: a corner disc can never be flipped, gates about 1.1 legal moves against an interior cell's 1.56, and is empty for most of the game, which is exactly what inflates a three-way probe. The interior-versus-border causal gap has a permutation p of 5e-05.

*What it needs:* the full 8x8 game and, ideally, the published Othello-GPT checkpoints, because "on 5x5 mini-Othello with a 205k-param model" is the sentence that lets the field ignore it. Per-cell error bars. And the writing should lead with the transferable methodological point rather than the Othello point: any headline intervention number reported without a norm-matched control and a collateral figure is reporting the norm, and this row has the table showing `strict` success climbing 0.379 → 0.756 alongside the control climbing 0.343 → 0.432 and the edit norm reaching 1.78x the residual itself.

**4. Fixed-budget MQAR frontiers measure plateau-escape time, not capacity — and the sub-quadratic curve people plot is often the guessing baseline.** `2026-07-25_zoology-mqar-recall` plus `2026-07-27_mqar-feature-map-vs-width`. Together these are a critique of how a widely used synthetic benchmark is read: the first shows that a smooth roughly-1/N "capacity decay" can be the query-ignoring chance floor to within ±0.04, and the second shows that a cliff which appears pinned across a 4x width sweep escapes at 8x budget at one width and flips from 0.176 to 0.975 on an init change at another. The constructive half is equally quotable — under the Taylor-exp kernel the frontier becomes a clean law, N* = d_head/4, doubling with every width doubling.

*What it needs:* this is the claim most exposed on scale, because it is a nano-scale critique of a benchmark that is run at 100M-plus parameters. It needs reproduction against zoology's and BASED's own configurations, or at minimum a budget-versus-scale contour showing where the artifact regime ends. It needs the escape-time-versus-(N, d, kernel) map that backlog item 51 already specifies, at five or more seeds. If the artifact regime extends to the scales the benchmark is actually used at, this is a genuinely useful correction to a live literature; if it does not, it is a small-model curiosity, and right now the repo cannot tell which.

**5. The shuffled-content gate.** `2026-07-29_mqar-gate-noise-control`. Identical parametrization, identical gradient dimensionality, identical activation statistics, wrong sequence's content: an exact no-op. Train-time-only decay-path noise: an exact no-op. This is a clean empirical refutation of the durable "gates are partly optimization devices" folklore for recall specifically, it gives the "gating is weighting" theory a controlled confirmation at 94k parameters, and the design transfers unchanged to any gated architecture. Its natural companion is `2026-07-26_mamba-mini-induction`, which finds that Mamba's own ablation ranking inverts on the selective-copy task the Mamba paper invented to motivate selectivity — Δ-selectivity buys nothing measurable while input-dependent B and C buy a lot, reconciled by the observation that Δ is a leak rate and B/C is an addressing mechanism.

*What it needs:* the shuffled-content control run at GLA or Mamba scale on language rather than only on MQAR at 94k parameters — that is a cheap experiment for anyone with a GPU and it is the whole ballgame for whether the result generalizes. More seeds. And the Mamba-inversion companion needs considerable care in the writing: at 60k parameters, 600 steps, one synthetic task, "Mamba's selectivity does nothing" is currently a more provocative sentence than the evidence supports, and the row's own caveat (the 350M language-modelling ablation it contradicts is measuring a different thing, and both can be true) should be in the abstract rather than the caveats.

**Honorable mentions that did not make the five.** `2026-07-26_data-repetition-epochs`' coverage confound — ninety percent of an apparent repetition penalty is corpus coverage — is the most immediately useful finding here for a practitioner, and it is a one-paragraph blog post rather than a paper. `2026-07-25_grokking-modular-addition`'s demonstration that the reported lead of spectral progress measures is a truncation-normalization artifact, complete with the sign-flipping re-analysis and a false-positive control, is the single sharpest refutation in the corpus but is aimed at a small literature.

## Closing: the four changes with the best return

First, **run the ten-times-steps arm.** It is the caveat on nearly every language row, it has been executed once, and that once overturned a headline. Pick the two or three rows whose conclusions would change most (`swiglu-vs-gelu-isoparam`, `head-dim-vs-count-isoparam`, the qknorm composite) and settle whether the repo is measuring fitting speed or quality.

Second, **run backlog item 51** before adding another MQAR row. The arc has known its primary instrument is miscalibrated since 07-28 and has built six experiments on top of it.

Third, **make the index honest.** The READMEs are consistently more careful than the titles and the registry `headline` fields. An outside reader meets the headline first, and the headlines currently claim more than the caveats permit.

Fourth, **require a stopping number when opening an arc.** The qknorm arc terminated cleanly because it had a 0.130-bpc denominator to refund. The MQAR arc did not, and has spent its last three nights decomposing residuals of residuals at one cell of one design space.
